The 4 Rules of SapaRemedy

How to permanently banish mid-month financial panic.

RULE 1

Give Every Naira a Job

Unassigned cash sitting in your main account gets eaten by impulse transfers. Assign every single Naira to an envelope until Left to Budget = ₦0.

RULE 2

Embrace Your True Expenses

Annual house rent, car servicing, and festive Owambe aren't emergencies—they are predictable. Break them into monthly envelopes now so they never shock you.

RULE 3

Roll With The Punches

Fuel price jumped or an urgent family request popped up? Don't abandon your budget! Use Move Money to shift funds from Lifestyle to cover it guilt-free.

RULE 4

Build Your Sapa Shield

Pay your future self first. Consistently fund your Emergency Shield & Treasury Bills envelope until you're spending last month's income today.
